Project ServicePanda.com.au 

Objective of the Project 
This Project Requires 2 App and 1 Admin 
1st App for Customer 
2nd App for Service Providers 
1 Admin to Manage all back end functionality 


First App for Customers app name will be ServicePanda 
Here customers sign up or sign in and enquire about types of services such as “domestic cleaning, end of lease or bond cleaning, carpet cleaning, pest control, gardening, removals, handyman, plumbing, electrician. 
Once Customers Sign in they will be able to see all different types of services
they can choose one types of service example: Domestic Cleaning system will request some specific information according to that type of service and request for a quote, 
this will send a request to all the service providers in that category in that area that a new quote is requested.
